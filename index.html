Protocol & metrics

Streaming input
Monitors see only observations and policy outputs available up to the current query. A rollout counts as detected once any alarm fires; thresholds are frozen on a disjoint calibration split.
bACC, Recall, TNR
Failed rollouts are positives. Recall is the share of failures with an alarm, TNR the share of successes without one, and bACC their mean.
MAE
Mean absolute gap between the first alarm and the human-reviewed onset, over detected failures only. Misses are reported through Recall.
bTWAon
Balanced time-weighted accuracy with the credit for each detected failure based on its distance to the annotated onset instead of its position in the rollout. Early and late alarms are penalised equally.
bTWAon = ½ · ( (1/N₊) · Σi∈TP 1 / (1 + (di/τ)²) + TNR ),   di = t̂i − ti*,   τ = 10 s
